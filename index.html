Four cameras, one demonstration

Each task was performed once and filmed by four synchronized GoPro cameras. The egocentric original is the reference demonstration; the other three show the same moment from a different viewpoint and form the benchmark's viewpoint axis. Clips are cut to the span of the original take and play in lockstep.
